      How to play

      1. You hear the first 0.1 seconds of a song.
      2. Guess it, or skip. Either way the clip grows: 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 seconds.
      3. Drag across the waveform to scrub anywhere inside what you have unlocked, then hit play.
      4. Seven tries. The sooner you get it, the better the share grid looks.
      5. Right artist, wrong song shows in yellow — you are close.
      6. For Anime and Game Music, naming the show or the game it is from counts too.

      Daily and Endless

      1. Daily is one song per genre a day. Everyone gets the same ones, and you get one go at each.
      2. Endless is yours: choose the genres and the difficulty, and keep playing.
      3. Your Music is a crate built from your Spotify top tracks and liked songs — connect it from the Endless picker.

      Keys

      1. Space plays the current clip.
      2. T switches theme, ? opens this box.
      3. On the waveform, arrows nudge the cue and Enter plays.

      Clips are Apple Music's public 30 second previews, so they usually start partway into a song rather than at the very first note.
